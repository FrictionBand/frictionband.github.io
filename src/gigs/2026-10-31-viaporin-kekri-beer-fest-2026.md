Viaporin Kekri will be celebrated on All Saints’ Day, October 31st 2026. Suomenlinna’s dark season urban event is now more local than ever – full of art, culture, and flavours!

Viaporin Kekri is a contemporary urban celebration inspired by old kekri traditions, created for the people of Suomenlinna, residents of the Helsinki metropolitan area, and visitors alike. The program includes light and darkness, music and movement, guided tours, film screenings, museum experiences, local food and drink – and much more!  
  
Come and experience the UNESCO World Heritage site of Suomenlinna in a new way and discover doors worth peeking behind. Prepare for an adventure with weather-appropriate gear and a curious mind!
